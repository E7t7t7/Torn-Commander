# Sell selected inventory through Torn Exchange
1. Open Trading and press **1. Scan inventory**.
2. Tick the items to sell (all owned units), or type smaller quantities. Search filters the pool; **Select all visible items** adds up to 20 item types. Zero excludes an item.
3. Choose **Highest return — any number of traders**, or set a maximum buyer count for fewer separate trades. Set your rating/online preferences.
4. Press **3. Scan Torn Exchange & find best return**. Wait for the scan-complete note; Stop keeps partial timestamped quotes.
5. Compare one complete buyer with the grouped split plan. Each trader's section lists quantities, prices and subtotal. Open their public price list to confirm, open their Torn trade page, or copy the plan.

Highest return is exact over the eligible offers actually scanned, not every trader on the internet. Discovery is bounded to 40 public listing rows per item plus up to 24 candidate price lists. Buyer-count-constrained splits use a 12-trader shortlist and can miss alternatives. Public advertised prices do not establish purchase capacity or guaranteed demand. Confirm price and quantity with each trader.

The total is sale proceeds. Your original acquisition costs are not known, so it is not labeled realized profit. No Torn API key/private quantities are sent to Torn Exchange. No items, money or trade offers are transferred automatically. Official inventory is profile-isolated; equipped, faction-owned and unique-stat equipment remain excluded from generic commodity quotes.

Public source: https://www.tornexchange.com/listings
