TORN COMMANDER — NATURAL NERVE GUIDE (CRIMES 2.0, 2026)
General reference only. Open Crime Coach and use Full Sync for your own account data.

CORE IDEA
=========
Natural Nerve Bar (NNB) is driven by hidden Crime Experience (CE), not by Crime Skill alone.

Natural nerve rises in 5-point steps. Crimes 2.0 no longer has the old 60-NNB ceiling.

A successful crime adds CE.
A normal failure hurts your crime chain but is not the same as a CE-destroying critical fail.
Critical failures are especially bad for a pure NNB grind because they can cost CE and reset chain progress.

For maximum long-term NNB, optimize:
safe CE gained per nerve
x crime progression bonuses
x crime-chain bonus
x total nerve throughput
while minimizing critical failures.

ACCOUNT-SPECIFIC PROGRESSION
============================
Use Crime Coach + Full Sync for current crime skill, nerve, merits, and
recommendations. Do not rely on another player's recorded values.

PHASE 1 — BUILD A SAFE CRIME CHAIN
==================================
Choose crimes appropriate to your own level and success probabilities.
Progress to more demanding crime categories as your account develops.

CRIME CHAIN
===========
Crime chain makes successful progression better and is worth protecting.

Cheap, safe crimes are valuable for chain building because each successful attempt contributes to the chain independent of nerve cost.

Concept:
safe cheap successes -> build chain -> expensive/high-CE safe crime

Hustling becomes particularly useful later because it combines low nerve cost with strong safety when your CE is ready.

PHASE 2 — ~35 TRUE NNB
=======================
Begin HUSTLING as the bridge.

Mix in SAFE Disposal opportunities.

Do not treat this as "must do every available job." Your target is safe CE accumulation, not maximum variety.

PHASE 3 — ~40 TRUE NNB
=======================
CRACKING becomes a major CE engine.

Before making it your life:
- obtain the Office Chair crime enhancer
- get the relevant Computer Science education running once addiction is controlled
- protect your crime chain
- avoid reckless targets that raise critical-failure risk

ENDGAME
=======
Do not replace the old "Warehouse Arsons forever" myth with "Cracking forever."

At high CE the correct philosophy becomes:
Use the hardest SAFE high-CE work you can perform consistently.

That may include:
- Cracking
- Disposal
- Scamming
- Forgery
- Arson
depending on your progression, skill, enhancers, education and current opportunity.

Your own account dashboard should track these rather than assume generic values.

NERVE THROUGHPUT
================
Natural regeneration is 1 nerve per 5 minutes while below maximum.

With a displayed max of 36, a completely empty bar caps in:
36 x 5 = 180 minutes = 3 hours.

That means low maximum nerve can cause a lot of wasted regeneration if you are away for long stretches.

Useful throughput sources can include:
- natural regen
- daily nerve refill
- alcohol
- company job-point nerve perks
- faction nerve / alcohol perks
- stock / event / newsletter sources when available

Spend boosted nerve back below the bar when practical so natural regeneration resumes.

TRUE NNB CALCULATOR
===================
Displayed max nerve can include bonuses.

Use Torn Commander's NNB tab:
Displayed max nerve
- faction flat nerve bonus
- merit flat nerve bonus
- company flat nerve bonus
- other flat nerve bonus
= natural value

If the result is not a multiple of 5, at least one bonus input is missing/wrong.
