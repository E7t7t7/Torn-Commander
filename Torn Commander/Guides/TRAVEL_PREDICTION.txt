TORN COMMANDER v0.5.1 - TRAVEL / FOREIGN STOCK

HOW TO GET USEFUL RESULTS
1. Open Travel. Choose your REAL carry capacity and flight method.
2. Choose whether the Mailing Yourself Abroad book bonus (-25% time) is active.
3. Leave cash budget at 0 for unlimited, or enter your real abroad-buying budget.
4. Keep the 5% Item Market fee enabled if you plan to sell through that market.
5. Select confidence/horizon, then Save + Refresh.
6. Search/filter to find routes; compare net/trip, net/hour, stock age, cash and ROI.
7. Confirm foreign stock in Torn before flying. Quotes are estimates, not reservations.

SOURCE POLICY
Commander uses the published DroqsDB public forecast and export APIs, which are
publicly documented for integrations. When remote predictions are unavailable,
Commander can infer local restock windows from its own observed public snapshots.
No scraping or unlocking TornTravel private subscription features.

FORECAST AND AVAILABILITY
- IN STOCK = reported by public snapshot at the displayed observation age; it can
  sell out before you land. Aged snapshots are marked STALE.
- PREDICTED = a modelled possible restock or flight window, not guaranteed.
- STALE / UNVERIFIED = delayed or incomplete source timestamps; confirm manually.
- Local restock estimates require at least three observed restock events (two
  complete cycle intervals), and are capped in confidence.
- Cached data is kept with the original refresh time during feed outages.

MATH AND LIMITATIONS
- Units = min(carry capacity, reported stock if in stock, affordable quantity).
- Cash required = units * foreign shop price.
- After-fee sell price = floor(Torn market value * .95) if fee enabled.
- Net/trip = units * (after-fee sell price - shop price) - standard roundtrip airfare.
- Net/hour = net/trip divided by estimated roundtrip flight duration + any wait
  until a future predicted departure. Ground-shopping/market-sale time excluded.
- ROI = net/trip / shop purchase budget.
- Travel durations use the Torn Wiki table; calculated route times are approximate.
- Business ticket opportunity cost is NOT deducted (its cash value varies).
- Personal mugging risk, rehab fees, market slippage, flight timing variation,
  outbound wait, time spent shopping, and all other external costs are excluded.
- Unknown price/route/stock status is not promoted as a profitable live run.

WINDOWS / TROUBLESHOOTING
The modern Commander dashboard is the normal reflowing, card-based view.
For deeper rows, switch to CLASSIC TOOLS -> Travel. If feeds fail, the UI shows
source errors and retains old data as stale. If public sources change their
response format, capture the error and report it for a parser update.
