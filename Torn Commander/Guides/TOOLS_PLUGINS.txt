TORN COMMANDER — TOOLS / PLUG-INS

Included launch catalog:
- TornTools
- Lazy Crimes
- Simple Cracking Helper
- torn.report
- YATA
- TornLabs Market

Torn Commander supports:
1) Local plain-JavaScript injection from its Scripts folder.
2) Unpacked Chromium/WebView2 extensions where supported by WebView2.
3) External userscript managers such as Greasemonkey/Violentmonkey for scripts that depend on GM_* APIs.

Unknown navigation can be allowed once or permanently through the personal domain whitelist. That whitelist does not bypass Torn rules, CAPTCHAs, Cloudflare, Google security, or browser restrictions.
