TORN COMMANDER — SHOPPING CART / BULK BUY

PURPOSE
Build a local cart from Torn's live Item Market data, then work through it one market purchase at a time.

ITEM SEARCH
You do NOT need Torn's exact punctuation anymore.
Examples:
  green paint
  Purple
  spray paint red
  Xanax

Use "Load item list" for autocomplete, or just type/paste a partial name and press Add / Find.
Commander resolves the text against Torn's live item catalog. Pasted rows with extra columns/totals are cleaned before matching.

PAINT PRESET
Choose the amount per color and press Load paint cart.
It loads Black, Blue, Green, Orange, Pink, Purple, Red, and White Spray Paint.

BUY LIMITS
Max price per unit:
  $0 = unlimited.
  Otherwise listings above this price are excluded.

Max total cart spend:
  $0 = unlimited.
  Otherwise Commander blocks a step that would push the recorded cart spend above the cap.

Minimum wallet to keep:
  $0 = allow spending cash down to $0.
  Otherwise Commander blocks a step that would leave less than this amount in your last-synced wallet.

NUKE MY PILE:
  Explicitly ignores all three Commander limits above.
  This does NOT bypass Torn and does NOT make Commander loop purchases.
  One Buy Next Once press still submits at most one market purchase.

PURCHASE FLOW
Scan / Build Cart:
  Reads the live market and builds the cheapest current plan.

Prepare Next:
  Opens the exact item market, finds the planned listing, opens Torn's Buy flow,
  prefills the planned quantity, and stops before the final confirmation.

Buy Next Once:
  Same preparation, then submits exactly one planned market purchase.

MANUAL PURCHASE TRACKING
The bundled Item Market Cart Helper watches the final purchase confirmation inside Commander.
When detected, Commander updates the Bought/Need quantities automatically.
If Torn changes its UI or a purchase is missed, select the row and use Record selected bought.
Undo last reverses the latest recorded cart purchase.

NOTE
Torn's user inventory API is cached by category (currently up to one hour), so Commander does not pretend
an inventory refresh can instantly prove a manual purchase. The browser helper is used for immediate tracking.

CHAT EXPORT
Select Copy cart for chat to place a clean plain-text request on the Windows clipboard.
Commander exports only quantities you still need, so already-recorded purchases are excluded.
If the cart has been scanned, the message also includes the current estimated market total.
Paste it directly into a Torn faction/private chat to ask for stock before market buying.
