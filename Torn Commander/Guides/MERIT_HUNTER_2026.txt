TORN COMMANDER — MERIT HUNTER 2026

Full Sync compares your achieved honors/medals against Torn's current honor and medal catalogs.

The grid now separates:
- Requirement: Torn's live catalog description.
- WhatToDo: Commander's plain-English instructions.
- Progress: only shown when Commander can map a trustworthy account statistic to that exact requirement.

Commander deliberately does NOT estimate referral or legacy-social honors from your own account level. A description such as "refer 3 players who reach level 10" is not 15/10 just because your account is level 15.

Useful examples:
- Zero Liability: allow ONE skimmer to collect at least 250 card details, then recover that same skimmer.
- Social Butterfly: personally refer 3 players and have each reach level 10.
- Pyramid Scheme: one of your referrals must refer another player, and that second-generation referral must reach level 10.
- The Socialist: the old Torn Lite/Facebook route is retired. At level 20 the honor can be purchased from the Points Building for 600 points.

Use Explain Selected for a focused explanation of any highlighted award. Guide Next opens the relevant Torn area and shows both the exact requirement and the action plan.
