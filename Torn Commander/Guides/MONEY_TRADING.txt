TORN COMMANDER — MONEY / TRADING GUIDE

PURPOSE
=======
Make money without turning the app into a bot.

Torn Commander should answer:
- What did I actually pay?
- What is my average cost?
- What do I expect to receive?
- What is the real profit?
- Is the ROI worth tying up my cash?
- How much cash must I keep untouched?

Use the Money tab as the durable ledger.

CORE RULES
==========
1. Record cost, not memory.
2. Separate "listed value" from "realized sale value."
3. Keep a minimum cash reserve.
4. Prefer repeatable margins over one lucky flip.
5. When comparing opportunities, consider ROI AND time-to-sell.
6. Never let a helper auto-buy or auto-sell for you.

YOUR EXISTING TRADING PREFERENCES
=================================
The project can later expose presets for:
- maximum stock purchase
- maximum item purchase
- minimum cash reserve
- minimum item profit per unit
- minimum ROI

These stay decision rules; purchase/sale clicks remain manual.
